## The winner: Canadian-made drone propulsion electronics

**What it is:** Canadian-designed and Canadian-built drone speed controllers (4-in-1 ESCs) and flight controllers, adding motors later. They're sold to Canadian drone makers, and to foreign defence prime contractors that must spend contract value in Canada (Canada's Industrial and Technological Benefits rules).

### Evidence

- **Shortage is documented:** U.S. Drone Dominance deliveries were delayed by a shortage of compliant motors, batteries, chips and speed controllers. Small motors cost **$12–25 from China versus $100–225 from U.S. makers** ([DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/08/03/american-drone-component-gap-commercial-fcc-january-2027/), [Inside Unmanned Systems](https://insideunmannedsystems.com/inside-the-drone-dominance-supply-chain-what-every-u-s-drone-company-must-source-and-by-when/)).
- **Forced demand (U.S.):** The Drone Dominance supply-chain framework phases in non-Chinese sourcing across **$6.6B** of small-drone procurement. Phase 2 requires motor assembly outside covered countries from August 2026. The FCC added foreign drones and critical components to its Covered List in December 2025 ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/12/fcc-adds-all-foreign-made-drones)).
- **Forced demand (Canada):**
  - The Defence Drone Initiative launched in July 2026 with a components stream ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/news/2026/07/government-of-canada-launches-defence-drone-initiative-to-strengthen-canadas-sovereign-defence-capabilities.html)).
  - The national objective is two million drones a year. About 400 companies qualified in the first intake, and the first six winners hold options up to 5,000 units ([Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/economy/article-ottawa-defence-drone-initiative-contracts-marketplace/), [AeroTime](https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/canada-drone-marketplace-six-systems-ukraine-defense-production)).
- **Canadian preference:** The Defence Industrial Strategy targets 70% domestic acquisition.
  - A "Canadian Company Boost" for firms with 70–100% Canadian content was reportedly released in May 2026 under the industrial-benefits reforms; this comes from a single secondary source ([MLT Aikins](https://www.mltaikins.com/insights/developments-in-canadas-defence-sector-mean-new-opportunities-for-businesses/), [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/bbe0ddfc/canada-s-defence-industrial-strategy-legal-considerations-for-businesses)).
  - Canadian suppliers also get a 10% bid-price reduction in strategic-sector procurements ([BLG](https://www.blg.com/en/insights/2026/02/how-canadas-defence-industrial-strategy-reshapes-defence-acquisition-and-procurement-law)).
- **Gap in Canada:** Industry says Canada lacks microelectronics, battery cells and permanent magnets for drone motors ([BetaKit](https://betakit.com/what-would-it-take-to-build-a-sovereign-drone-industry-in-canada/)). Repeated searches found **no Canadian maker of drone motors, ESCs or flight controllers**. That is absence of evidence, not proof, so verify it.
- **Allied demand beyond Canada:** China has restricted drone-part exports to the West, and Europe and Ukraine can't yet replace Chinese supply at scale ([CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/why-chinas-uav-supply-chain-restrictions-weaken-ukraines-negotiating-power), [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/04/chinas-sanctions-hit-europes-emerging-drone-doctrine/)). Canada and Ukraine announced joint drone production on September 10, 2026 ([PMO](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/09/10/canada-and-ukraine-scale-drone-production-build-canadas-defence)).
- **Building blocks exist:**
  - Firmware: AM32 ESC firmware is open source under GPLv3, so there are no licence fees.
  - Magnets: Neo Performance Materials (a Toronto-headquartered company) began commercial magnet production in Estonia in September 2026. Its feedstock is partly Russian, so qualify it carefully.
  - Testing: Tyto Robotics in Quebec makes propulsion test stands.
- **Non-dilutive funding:** About $900M to the National Research Council, including a defence-focused IRAP stream ([BetaKit](https://betakit.com/ottawa-gives-nrc-900-million-for-drone-innovation-hub-and-defence-focused-irap-stream/)); the BDC $6B Defence Platform; and National Defence's Drone Surge challenge, worth up to $350k ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/programs/defence-ideas/element/contests/challenge/drone-surge-scaling-tactical-uas-manufacturing-modern-defence.html)).

### Competitors

- **U.S.:** Unusual Machines (Brave ESC/FC, on the Blue UAS list), Lumenier, KDE Direct, Hoverfly Elements (motors and ESCs, with a Korean partner), ARK Electronics, ModalAI.
- **Europe:** Orqa (Croatia), Arctic RAVN (Finland).
- **Canada:** none found. The allied U.S. and European makers can still sell in Canada, but they don't earn the Canadian-content credit.

The score rose from a first-pass 8.0 to 8.55 as evidence came in: the 400-supplier marketplace, the Canadian Company Boost, the 10% bid preference, and industrial-benefits obligations.

### What would drop it below 8

- Drone makers say they'll stay with U.S. or Chinese parts and that Canadian content isn't scored at the component level.
- A funded Canadian competitor already exists.
- Neither founder can design power electronics and firmware, or hire someone who can.
- Defence budgets or policy reverse.

## Plan

1. **Days 0–30 (<$2k):**
   - Interview 20 of the ~400 qualified drone suppliers. Ask which components they can't source as compliant or Canadian, and at what price and volume.
   - Confirm the Canadian Company Boost text and the Defence Drone Initiative component rules on canada.ca.
2. **Days 30–60:** Apply to IRAP's defence stream and Drone Surge. Check whether you need Controlled Goods Program registration.
3. **Months 2–6:**
   - Design a 4-in-1 ESC: STM32 microcontroller, Infineon/onsemi/TI power parts, AM32 firmware, assembled at a Canadian contract manufacturer.
   - Bench-test on Tyto stands and deliver samples to 5 customers.
4. **Months 6–18:** Add a flight controller (PX4/ArduPilot), then motors using non-Chinese magnets.
